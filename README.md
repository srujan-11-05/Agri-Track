# 🌾 AgriTrack

> **Complete Agricultural Business Management Platform**
> 
> A comprehensive web-based platform for farmers, suppliers, and agribusiness managers to efficiently manage crops, inventory, sales, expenses, and farm operations from a single dashboard.

---

## ⭐ Key Features at a Glance

```
✅ User Authentication       ✅ Crop Management
✅ Farm Profiles            ✅ Inventory Tracking
✅ Sales Recording          ✅ Expense Management
✅ Dashboard Analytics      ✅ Activity Planning
```

---

## 🎯 What AgriTrack Solves

### **Before AgriTrack** ❌
- Scattered farm data across multiple platforms
- Manual record keeping and calculations
- No real-time visibility into farm operations
- Difficult expense and profit tracking
- Time-consuming paperwork

### **With AgriTrack** ✅
- All farm data in one centralized platform
- Automatic calculations and analytics
- Real-time dashboard overview
- Instant profit/loss visibility
- Digital record keeping

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. **🔐 Secure User Management**
- Email-based registration with strong password hashing
- Role-based access (Farmer, Supplier, Manager)
- JWT token authentication
- Session management

### 2. **👨‍🌾 Farmer Profile & Farm Information**
- Complete farm details (name, location, size)
- Contact information management
- Crop type specifications
- Profile editing and updates

### 3. **🌱 Crop Planning & Seasonal Activities**
- Add and manage multiple crops
- Track planting and harvest dates
- Monitor crop status (growing, ready, harvested)
- Schedule and track seasonal activities
- Activity completion monitoring

### 4. **📦 Inventory Management**
- Track seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, equipment
- Quantity and unit management
- Purchase date and cost tracking
- Inventory categorization
- Total inventory valuation

### 5. **💰 Sales & Purchase Recording**
- Quick and easy sales entry
- Buyer information tracking
- Automatic total price calculation
- Sales history and reporting
- Revenue analytics

### 6. **💸 Expense & Income Tracking**
- Categorized expense logging (10+ categories)
- Daily expense tracking
- Expense analytics by category
- Profit/loss calculation
- Profit margin analysis

### 7. **📊 Dashboard Analytics**
- Key metrics overview (crops, sales, expenses, profit)
- Real-time profit calculation
- Performance indicators
- Quick statistics summary
- Data-driven insights

### 8. **📅 Activity Planning**
- Seasonal activity scheduling
- Activity status tracking (pending, completed)
- Upcoming activities overview
- Historical activity records
